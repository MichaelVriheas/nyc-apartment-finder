How to read these insights

Median asking rent: The middle advertised base rent among apartments south of the approximate George Washington Bridge approach captured in a saved search. It excludes utilities, fees, and apartments outside your budget. A subgroup needs at least three records before its median is displayed.

Trends: Only checks made with the same RentCast search methodology are plotted together. When the search methodology changes, the timeline starts a new comparison series rather than connecting incompatible datasets. No missing weeks are invented.

Availability: RentCast's reported active status may be outdated. Each search query can contain up to 500 records, so coverage is incomplete when capped. Neighborhood assignments follow NYC's 2020 statistical areas with approximate local splits, and exclude all listings north of the GW Bridge approach cutoff.
